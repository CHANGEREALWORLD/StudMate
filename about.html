ℹ️ About StudMate

📘 What is StudMate?

StudMate is a smart academic and career organizer designed to help students plan, track, and achieve their educational and professional goals efficiently.

🎯 Purpose

This project was created to help students manage their time, track performance, and plan careers — all in one easy-to-use platform.

👩‍🎓 Who is it for?

StudMate is perfect for college and university students who want a productivity boost while preparing for exams, improving skills, and tracking goals.

🧩 Key Features

  • 🗓️ Daily, Weekly, and Subject Planners
  • 📊 SGPA/CGPA/GPA Calculators
  • 🎯 Career and Skill Tracker
  • 📅 Time Table Generator
  • 📅 Monthly Table Generator
